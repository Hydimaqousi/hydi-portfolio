Responsible AI by Design

Governance is part of the product.

OpsGuide is designed as a retrieval and guidance layer—not a replacement for authoritative policy or professional judgment.

Source-grounded response policy

Operational guidance should be produced only when sufficient evidence exists in approved sources. If reliable evidence is unavailable, the system should refuse to invent an answer and route the employee to related resources or human escalation.

Every substantive operational answer includes a traceable source.
Exact page or section references appear only when reliably known.
Superseded or retired content is excluded from normal retrieval.
Cross-domain questions trigger clarification instead of assumption.
Potential source conflicts are surfaced rather than silently resolved.
No student PII is included in the portfolio prototype.
01

Verified Answer

Evidence is sufficient and current. The response is summarized and paired with source attribution.

02

Review Recommended

Relevant information exists, but the scenario requires additional context, cross-domain review, or employee verification.

03

No Verified Answer

The approved library does not support a reliable answer. The product presents related resources and escalation instead.
