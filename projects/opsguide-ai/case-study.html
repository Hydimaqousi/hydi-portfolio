01 • Discovery

The problem is not missing information. It is finding the right information.

Operational guidance can be distributed across PowerPoints, policies, job aids, FAQs, emails, training material, process timelines, and departmental references. Employees may spend unnecessary time determining where to look, verifying whether guidance is current, or escalating routine questions to experienced staff.

Frontline friction

Employees need an answer while completing real work, but often must search multiple resources before locating the authoritative guidance.

Onboarding burden

New employees must learn both the process and where a large number of resources live before becoming self-sufficient.

Knowledge risk

Duplicate, outdated, or differently worded resources can create inconsistent interpretation and unnecessary escalation.

02 • Product Strategy

Make approved knowledge easier to retrieve, verify, and apply.

The product is intentionally positioned as a retrieval and guidance layer. Authoritative documents remain the source of truth.

1. AskEmployee submits a natural-language operational question.
2. RetrieveSystem searches only the approved knowledge library.
3. SynthesizeAI creates a concise answer supported by evidence.
4. VerifySource, metadata, and direct link are shown with the answer.
5. Act / EscalateEmployee continues the workflow or escalates when evidence is insufficient.

Product vision

Enable employees to find, understand, verify, and apply approved operational knowledge within seconds rather than searching across fragmented resources.

Product principle

AI provides navigation and synthesis. Authoritative documentation provides truth.

03 • Users

Designed around the employee knowledge journey.

New Employee

Needs guided access to reliable resources without memorizing where every document is stored.

Experienced Employee

Needs rapid retrieval for uncommon or cross-domain scenarios while handling active work.

Trainer / Senior

Needs fewer repeat information-location questions and more time for genuinely complex coaching.

Knowledge Owner

Needs document ownership, freshness, usage, feedback, conflict, and knowledge-gap visibility.

Manager — Future

Needs aggregate insight into search friction, unanswered topics, and areas requiring training or documentation improvement.

Compliance Stakeholder

Needs traceable evidence, version control, and a design that does not silently manufacture policy guidance.

Concept Architecture

How the intended workflow fits together.

The current public prototype is static, but it demonstrates the user experience and decision logic that a governed production system could support.

1. Advisor QuestionNatural-language operational question
2. Intent & ContextIdentify scenario, effective date, and missing inputs
3. Approved KnowledgeRetrieve only governed, permissioned sources
4. Source HierarchyPrioritize current federal authority and institutional implementation
5. Guided AnswerQuick answer, advisor checks, reasoning, and citations
6. Verify or EscalateOpen evidence, clarify uncertainty, or route to a human
Prototype boundary: V0.5 uses curated, modeled responses based on public Federal Student Aid / StudentAid.gov and UMGC resources. It does not currently perform live retrieval, connect to student systems, or make autonomous eligibility decisions.
04 • MVP Requirements

Functional requirements with testable acceptance criteria.

The MVP deliberately focuses on retrieval, evidence, verification, clarification, and escalation before considering workflow automation.

IDRequirementAcceptance Criteria
FR-001Natural-language queryUser can submit free-form text; empty queries are blocked; submitted question is preserved in the interaction.
FR-002Approved-source retrievalSearch is limited to indexed approved sources; evidence is traceable; superseded content is excluded from standard retrieval.
FR-003Concise answer generationResponse answers the question using retrieved evidence, avoids unsupported procedural claims, and distinguishes general guidance from case-specific decisions.
FR-004Mandatory source attributionEvery substantive operational answer includes a source. No citation = incomplete response.
FR-005Source locationSection/page/slide is displayed only when reliably known; the product never fabricates document locations.
FR-006Cross-domain detectionWhen multiple operational domains may apply, the product identifies them rather than collapsing them into one rule.
FR-007Clarifying questionsIf missing context materially changes the answer, the user is prompted for the relevant funding/process context.
FR-008Insufficient-evidence stateThe system refuses to invent an answer and offers related resources, query refinement, or human escalation.
FR-009Conflict handlingPotentially conflicting sources are surfaced with metadata and escalated when governance rules cannot establish precedence.
FR-010Source freshness metadataSource records can contain owner, type, effective/review date, version, status, authority level, URL, and supersession fields.
FR-011User feedbackEvery answer supports Helpful / Not Helpful feedback for later quality analysis.
FR-012Knowledge-gap detectionFailed retrieval topics can be logged and summarized for documentation/process improvement.

06 • Responsible AI

Governance is a product requirement, not an afterthought.

Grounded responses

Operational answers should be based on approved retrieved evidence rather than general model knowledge presented as institutional policy.

Human verification

Source links and metadata remain visible so the employee can validate current guidance before acting.

Refusal behavior

If the knowledge library cannot support a reliable answer, OpsGuide should say so instead of hallucinating.

Conflict visibility

Potential conflicts between sources are surfaced rather than silently resolved without a governance rule.

Privacy boundary

The portfolio prototype contains no student PII and does not connect to student information systems.

Source lifecycle

Future enterprise implementation would incorporate ownership, review schedules, superseded status, and access permissions.

What I Would Build Next

From portfolio prototype to controlled enterprise pilot.

Governed Retrieval

Replace modeled answers with permission-aware RAG over approved content, including effective dates, source ownership, review status, and superseded-document controls.

Advisor Workflow

Add guided scenario inputs, role-based access, reusable decision paths, feedback capture, and escalation workflows for questions that require human judgment.

Analytics & Governance

Measure time-to-answer, citation validity, unresolved searches, content gaps, source conflicts, advisor helpfulness, adoption, and pilot-level operational impact.

09 • Risk Management

Major risks are identified before automation expands.

Hallucination

Impact: Critical. Mitigate through retrieval grounding, mandatory citations, and refusal behavior.

Outdated policy

Impact: High. Mitigate through version metadata, source owners, review dates, and superseded status.

Conflicting guidance

Impact: High. Mitigate through authority hierarchy, conflict warnings, and escalation.

Overreliance on AI

Impact: High. Keep source verification directly in the employee workflow.

Sensitive data

Impact: Critical. Future production use would require role-based access, privacy controls, and approved enterprise architecture.

Weak documentation

Impact: Medium/High. Failed searches become knowledge-gap signals for content owners.

11 • Prototype Testing & Iteration

Early mobile UAT informed the next design iteration.

The prototype was tested on a live GitHub Pages deployment using realistic employee questions. The first round validated the end-to-end concept and also surfaced mobile UX improvements.

Passed

Federal-aid withdrawal scenario returned source-grounded R2T4 guidance with supporting source cards, source metadata, and direct verification links.

Observed

The initial answer was too text-heavy on mobile, making it slower to scan during an active employee workflow.

Iteration

V0.3 introduces a short Quick Answer, optional detail expansion, tighter mobile wrapping, and overflow corrections while preserving evidence visibility.
