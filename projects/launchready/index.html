The challenge

Replace fragmented onboarding with one controlled operating workflow.

Customer

A fictional 475-employee organization operating across eight locations and hiring approximately 25–35 employees per month.

Problem

Email requests, eight spreadsheets, disconnected checklists, missing information, unclear ownership, and limited reporting.

Pilot decision

Launch at two locations first, then use test evidence, support trends, and Day-30 measures to guide expansion.

Data and testing

The project uses intentional errors and defects to demonstrate real analysis.

Data validation

Invalid emails, duplicates, missing start dates and managers, inconsistent locations, and nonstandard dates are identified and corrected.

UAT evidence

Fifteen end-to-end scenarios connect requirements, expected results, actual results, defects, retests, and exit criteria.

Launch governance

The pilot proceeds with zero open critical/high defects and one accepted medium workaround.

Sample customer data includes intentional errors so correction and validation decisions can be explained in an interview.
UAT results: 14 of 15 scenarios passed, a 93% pass rate, with no open critical or high-severity defects.
