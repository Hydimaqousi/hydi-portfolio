OpsGuide AI — Source-Grounded Decision Support for Financial Aid Operations

Concept Creator · Product & Project Lead

Designed to reduce search friction, strengthen source verification, and support more consistent advisor decision-making.

Product ManagementApplied AIBusiness AnalysisProcess ImprovementUXKnowledge Management

Why It Matters

When guidance is fragmented, advisors spend time searching instead of resolving work, new hires depend heavily on senior staff, and inconsistent interpretation can create operational risk.

Problem

Employees can lose time searching across fragmented policies, job aids, presentations, FAQs, and training resources simply to determine where authoritative guidance lives.

Product Approach

I translated the operational pain point into an advisor-focused MVP with natural-language retrieval, source hierarchy, transparent calculations, effective-date awareness, clarification for missing inputs, conflict handling, human escalation, and measurable pilot targets.

Current output: Interactive V0.5 prototype + product strategy + advisor scenarios + transparent calculations + source-governance model + UAT concepts + pilot success measures + roadmap.
